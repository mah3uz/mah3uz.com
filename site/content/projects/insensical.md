## Why another multiplexer

Terminal multiplexers are wonderful and ancient. They live inside a terminal emulator, which means two layers of
keybindings, two ideas of what a window is, and a lot of configuration to make them agree. Insensical is the
multiplexer *as* the window: splits, tabs and sessions handled natively.

## Built for agents

More and more terminal sessions aren't typed by people. AI coding agents run for minutes or hours, in parallel, and
you need to watch several at once. Insensical is designed with that workflow in mind.

## Get it

Releases are published on GitHub and through Homebrew.
