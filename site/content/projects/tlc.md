## The engagement

TLC is a long-term client, and the work covers the whole platform. As an engineer I build and improve the web application.
As an administrator I look after what it runs on: configuring and maintaining the servers, automating deployments, keeping
software patched, and making sure backups exist and restore.

## What a long partnership is worth

- **Continuity.** Knowing a system's history — why a setting is the way it is, which part was fragile three years ago —
  makes every change safer and faster than it would be for someone arriving fresh.
- **One person who sees both sides.** Many problems live in the gap between application and infrastructure: a slow page
  that is really a database configuration, a failed deploy that is really disk space. Owning both closes the gap.
- **Reliability first.** New features never come at the expense of a stable, secure production system.
- **No surprises.** Plain-language communication about what changed, why, and what's next.
