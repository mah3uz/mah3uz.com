## The engagement

TLC is a long-term client. I help build and look after their web platform: shipping new features, keeping the
production system healthy, and making sure the people who depend on it every day aren't let down.

## What a long engagement looks like

- **Continuity.** Knowing a system's history makes every change safer and faster.
- **Reliability first.** New features never come at the expense of a stable production platform.
- **Clear communication.** Plain-language updates on what changed, why, and what's next.

Client details stay confidential; [get in touch](/about#contact) to talk about a project of your own.
