## The engagement

TourHub is a web platform for the tour industry, and an ongoing client of mine. The work spans both halves of what I do.
On the engineering side, that means building and refining product features. On the operations side, it means the
environments those features travel through: a staging environment that mirrors production closely enough to be trusted,
and a production platform that people depend on during their working day.

## How I approach a live platform

- **Staging that tells the truth.** A staging environment is only useful if it behaves like production — same
  configuration shape, same kind of data, same deploy process. Every change is reviewed there before it ships.
- **Small, frequent releases.** Shipping in small increments keeps each change easy to reason about, easy to verify and
  easy to roll back. Large, infrequent releases concentrate risk; small ones spread it thin.
- **Reliability is a feature.** New work never comes at the expense of a stable platform. Monitoring, backups and the
  ability to recover quickly matter as much as the features users can see.
- **Clear communication.** Plain-language updates about what changed, why, and what comes next.

The specifics of client work stay with the client. If you're running a platform that needs both careful engineering and a
steady hand on its infrastructure, [let's talk](/about#contact).
