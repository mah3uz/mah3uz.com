## The engagement

TourHub is a web platform for the tour industry, and an ongoing client of mine. My work spans the product end to end:
building features, reviewing them in a staging environment that mirrors production, and releasing them to the people
who rely on the platform every day.

## How I work with clients

- **Staging first.** Every change is reviewed in an environment that behaves like production before it ships.
- **Small, frequent releases.** Shipping in small steps keeps risk low and feedback fast.
- **Ownership.** I treat a client's platform with the same care as my own products.

The details of client work stay with the client; [get in touch](/about#contact) if you'd like to hear more about how
I could help with yours.
