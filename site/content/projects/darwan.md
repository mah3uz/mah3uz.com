## Two gates

*Darwan* means doorkeeper. Every Linux desktop has two gates: the login screen when the machine starts, and the lock
screen when you step away. They're usually themed separately, by hand, in two different systems. Darwan treats them
as one design problem.

## What it does

- **Choose** from 41 hand-crafted themes.
- **Configure** each one — colours, wallpaper and layout.
- **Preview** the result before touching your system.
- **Apply** it to SDDM and to your Quickshell lock screen in one step.

Built in QML on Qt and Quickshell, for Arch Linux and Hyprland setups.
