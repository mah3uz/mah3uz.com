## TLC

[TLC — Total Lifestyle Credit](https://tlc.com.au) is a Sydney-based lender, licensed under the Australian Credit
Licence, that offers personal loans and payment plans for the costs people would rather not put off: medical and
cosmetic procedures, dental work, IVF, weddings, vehicles, veterinary care and more, from $2,001 to $70,000. Over
193,000 Australians have applied through it.

## What I built

- **The CRM.** TLC runs on a CRM I built in **Laravel**: the system where applications arrive and customers and
  their loans are looked after, built around how TLC's team actually works.
- **A broker API.** Loan applications don't only come from TLC's own website. I designed and documented a
  token-authenticated JSON API that lets brokers and partner businesses submit applications straight into the CRM. It
  is strict about its input — every field validated, every list of options fixed and case-sensitive — so what arrives
  is ready to assess, and a partner sees exactly what was wrong when it isn't.
- **The infrastructure.** As the administrator, I configure and maintain the servers it runs on, automate deployments,
  keep software patched, and make sure backups exist and restore. In a business that handles personal and financial
  information, that part isn't optional.

## What a long partnership is worth

- **Continuity.** Since 2020 I've known the system's history — why a setting is the way it is, which part was fragile
  three years ago — and that makes every change safer and faster than it would be for someone arriving fresh.
- **One person who sees both sides.** Many problems live in the gap between application and infrastructure: a slow page
  that is really a database configuration, a failed deploy that is really disk space. Owning both closes the gap.
- **Reliability first.** New features never come at the expense of a stable, secure production system.
- **No surprises.** Plain-language communication about what changed, why, and what's next.
